Penguin flipper length and body mass

Author

Cesar Figueroa

Published

September 24, 2026

Question

Do penguins with longer flippers tend to have greater body mass? You wouldn’t be surprise once you know the answer.

Data source

Data: Palmer Penguins, collected by Dr. Kristen Gorman and Palmer Station Antarctica LTER. The data are available under a CC0 license.

Check data quality

I check for missing values in flipper length and body mass before examining their relationship.

measurements <- penguins[c("flipper_length_mm", "body_mass_g")]

nrow(measurements)
[1] 344
colSums(is.na(measurements))
flipper_length_mm       body_mass_g 
                2                 2

Prepare the data

I retain observations with both flipper length and body mass available. Missing values in other variables do not affect this selection.

measurements_clean <- measurements[complete.cases(measurements), ]

nrow(measurements_clean)
[1] 342
nrow(measurements) - nrow(measurements_clean)
[1] 2

Visualize the relationship

Each point represents one penguin with both measurements available.

plot(
  measurements_clean$flipper_length_mm,
  measurements_clean$body_mass_g,
  xlab = "Flipper length (mm)",
  ylab = "Body mass (g)",
  pch = 19,
  col = "steelblue"
)

Measure the association

I calculate Pearson’s correlation to summarize the linear association between flipper length and body mass.

correlation <- cor(
  measurements_clean$flipper_length_mm,
  measurements_clean$body_mass_g
)

round(correlation, 3)
[1] 0.871

Findings :O

Among the 342 penguins with both measurements available, flipper length and body mass have a Pearson correlation of 0.871. This indicates a strong positive linear association, consistent with the upward pattern in Figure 1.

This analysis combines three species. Differences between species may contribute to the overall association, so the result does not necessarily describe the relationship within each species. Correlation does not establish that longer flippers cause greater body mass. Two observations with missing measurements were excluded.
